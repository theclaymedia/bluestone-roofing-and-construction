Our story

Born over a decade ago, from a family of builders

Our founder began as a roofing contractor, following a family legacy of general contractors.

After framing hundreds of homes to prepare the surfaces for a great roof, he decided to expand his services to the commercial and residential markets, adding more services such as inspections, repairs, gutter installs and much more.

Blue Stone Roofing is now one of the local leaders within Snohomish and King County when it comes to roofing projects. We are always ready to listen and intentionally generate great solutions.

Our warranty

Blue Shield Protection

Our warranty program begins as soon as you sign with us.

Emergency repair

Our 24 hour emergency new-leak repair service is at your disposal from the minute you schedule your new roof.

Damage protection

We will repair any damage to the inside or outside of your home at no cost, in the case of any faulty workmanship.

Maintenance

We visit your home once during the first 18 months after your new roof installation to inspect it and clean all loose debris from the roof surface and gutters.

Service calls

Make service calls at no cost during the labor warranty period, whether a storm blows, a tree falls, or a pesky rodent nests in your roof. We supply inspection reports and photos to help with any claims.

Before the job

Leak cover

If you’re already contracted with us but your roof springs a leak before we start your roof replacement project, we will stop the leak with a repair or tarp over the leak area at no additional cost.

After the job

Inspections

The Shield of Protection means that we’ll return after a year to air-blow your new roof, inspect the roof condition and recommend a roof cleaning schedule for you.

After the job

Repairs

If a leak were to happen under the Shield of Protection period due to faulty workmanship, we will fix the leak and any subsequent damage.

Manufacturer warranties

All material manufacturers have their own warranty options available. As a certified contractor with the manufacturers we work with, Blue Stone can provide their best available warranties and will register them for you, ensuring that you and your new roof are protected.

Malarkey Roofing Products
As a selected Emerald Premium Contractor, we provide enhanced product warranties when installing Malarkey shingles and accessories as part of a Secure Choice™ Roof System.
CertainTeed Roofing
A Lifetime Limited Warranty on most residential roofing materials, with full coverage for materials and labor during the initial SureStart™ period and prorated protection thereafter.
Nu-Ray Metals
A 40 year non-prorated finish warranty on 26 and 24 gauge lifetime standing seam clip fastener metal panel roofing systems.
WeatherBond TPO/PVC
Up to a Limited Lifetime Material Warranty for 60 mil and thicker membranes, and up to 15 to 30 years for standard systems.
Flex Roofing Systems
A Residential Lifetime Material Warranty on select PVC membranes when installed according to manufacturer specifications.

Our process

How a roof replacement runs

  1. Inspection and consultation

    Our team evaluates your current roof and recommends the best replacement options.

  2. Material selection

    Choose from top-quality materials suited to your budget, style, and performance needs.

  3. Professional installation

    Our certified technicians ensure precise, efficient installation with minimal disruption.

  4. Final inspection and cleanup

    We guarantee a safe, clean, and fully inspected finished roof that meets industry standards.

Roof replacement projects typically take about 2 to 3 days to complete. Other exterior work may take anywhere from 2 to 7 days, depending on the scope and complexity of the job.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Something we didn’t cover? Ask us directly.

Are you a licensed contractor?

Yes, we are a licensed roofing contractor in the State of Washington, operating under license number BLUESSR793QS.

Do you have worker’s compensation and liability insurance?

We are fully insured and carry workers’ compensation coverage through the Washington State Department of Labor and Industries (L&I). Our L&I account number is 604-805-821.

Can you provide references from previous jobs?

Please read the reviews from our satisfied customers, or contact our office for a list of current references.

What’s included in your estimate?

During their visit to your home, our estimator will inspect your current roof and provide you with a detailed proposal outlining the full scope of work via email.

What warranties do you provide for our new roof?

We use high-quality roofing materials from the most trusted manufacturers in the industry. Each manufacturer offers specific product warranties to ensure maximum protection for your home. In addition, we back our work with our own labor warranty of up to 10 years.

Do you provide a temporary roof leak solution until the new roof is installed?

As a courtesy to our customers, we will provide temporary leak protection on your existing roof at no additional cost. This service will help safeguard your home from potential water intrusion and give you peace of mind until our roofing professionals install your new roof.

How long will the project take?

Once we receive your signed proposal, we will provide you with an estimated start date for your project. Please note that this date is approximate and may change due to unforeseen factors such as weather conditions or scheduling adjustments. Once the project begins, we will give you a more accurate timeline. Roof replacement projects typically take about 2 to 3 days to complete, while other exterior work may take anywhere from 2 to 7 days, depending on the scope and complexity of the job.

How will additional charges be handled?

Our onsite manager or office staff will stay in communication with you throughout the project to keep you updated on any changes to the original proposal. If modifications are needed, we will provide a written or verbal explanation detailing the changes, associated costs, and a request for your approval. Once your approval is received, any additional charges will be added to the final invoice.
